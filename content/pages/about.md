    # Engineering Flight. Building Possibilities.


    Aerial Martial is the Aeromodelling Club of PES's Modern College of Engineering, Pune, established in the academic year 2016–17. Built around a shared interest in aviation, aircraft design, and practical engineering, the club provides a platform for students to explore the science and technology of flight through the design, development, fabrication, and testing of unmanned aerial vehicles.


    Our work extends beyond the construction of model aircraft. We approach aeromodelling as an integrated engineering discipline, bringing together concepts from aerodynamics, structural mechanics, propulsion, electronics, and flight-control systems. From the initial stages of conceptualisation to the development of a functional flying platform, every project presents an opportunity to translate theoretical understanding into practical engineering.


    The club primarily works with fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicles and drones, exploring different aircraft configurations, design approaches, and engineering challenges. Through these projects, members gain exposure to the interconnected systems that make an aircraft capable of controlled, stable, and purposeful flight.


    At Aerial Martial, learning is driven by the process of building. Aircraft design involves more than arriving at a final configuration; it requires evaluating ideas, understanding constraints, making informed engineering decisions, and continuously improving through experimentation. Students engage with the challenges of turning design concepts into physical structures, integrating onboard systems, and preparing aircraft for testing and operation.


    Beyond the workshop, Aerial Martial actively participates in technical competitions and engineering challenges, including SAEISS Design, Development and Competition (DDC). These platforms place engineering knowledge in a competitive environment, encouraging teams to develop solutions that satisfy technical requirements while demonstrating design capability, execution, and teamwork. The club has also secured competitive rankings through its participation in such events.


    The experience of working within Aerial Martial extends beyond individual technical specialisations. Members collaborate across different aspects of aircraft development, share knowledge, solve problems collectively, and learn to approach engineering challenges with discipline and accountability. Experienced members contribute to the development of newer team members, helping sustain technical knowledge and strengthen the club's engineering culture.


    Since its establishment, Aerial Martial has remained a space where curiosity about flight is transformed into practical engineering experience. It brings together students who are willing to question, design, build, test, and improve — working towards aircraft that are not merely conceived on paper, but engineered to take flight.


    **We don't just study flight. We engineer the machines that make it possible.**

  # From Concept to Flight


  Aerial Martial's activities encompass the different engineering disciplines involved in the development of unmanned aircraft. The club brings together design, fabrication, system integration, and testing under a common objective: developing functional aircraft through practical engineering.


  Our scope of operation extends across the following areas:


  ## 01. Aircraft Design & Configuration


  The development of an aircraft begins with translating its intended purpose into a suitable design configuration. The team explores aircraft layouts, geometric parameters, aerodynamic considerations, and structural requirements to establish the foundation of each project.


  This stage involves understanding the relationship between aircraft geometry, expected performance, stability, and operational requirements. Design decisions are evaluated with consideration for the practical constraints of manufacturing, available resources, and intended applications.


  ## 02. Aerodynamics & Performance Considerations


  Aerodynamics plays a fundamental role in determining how an aircraft behaves in flight. The team engages with the principles governing lift, drag, stability, and control to understand and evaluate aircraft performance.


  From wing geometry and airfoil selection to configuration-level considerations, aerodynamic understanding informs the development process and helps guide design decisions. The objective is to create aircraft configurations that are suited to their intended operating requirements.


  ## 03. Structural Design & Fabrication


  A successful aircraft must translate its design into a physical structure capable of withstanding operational loads while maintaining functional integrity.


  The club engages in the development and fabrication of aircraft structures, considering material selection, component geometry, structural arrangements, and manufacturing feasibility. Fabrication and assembly provide members with practical experience in transforming digital concepts and engineering drawings into physical airframes.


  ## 04. Propulsion & Power Systems


  Propulsion is central to an aircraft's ability to generate the thrust required for flight. The team works with the considerations involved in selecting and integrating propulsion components and associated power systems.


  Understanding the relationship between propulsion requirements, aircraft configuration, power availability, and overall performance is an important part of developing a functional aerial platform.


  ## 05. Avionics, Electronics & Flight Control


  Modern unmanned aircraft depend on the coordinated operation of onboard electronic and control systems.


  This area encompasses the integration of relevant electronics, onboard components, control hardware, and flight-related systems. The objective is to understand how individual components interact to support the operation and controllability of an aircraft.


  System integration requires careful consideration of compatibility, electrical arrangements, component placement, and operational reliability.


  ## 06. Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Development


  Aerial Martial works across different categories of unmanned aerial platforms, with a focus on fixed-wing UAVs and drones.


  These aircraft present different design and engineering challenges, ranging from airframe configuration and aerodynamic efficiency to propulsion arrangements, system integration, and flight-control requirements.


  Working across these platforms allows members to explore different approaches to aerial engineering while developing a broader understanding of unmanned aircraft technologies.


  ## 07. Testing, Validation & Flight Operations


  Aircraft development does not conclude with fabrication and assembly. Testing is essential to evaluating whether the developed system performs as intended.


  The club's engineering process includes the preparation and evaluation of aircraft systems, identification of potential issues, and refinement based on observations and testing outcomes. Ground-level checks and flight-related evaluations provide opportunities to assess the interaction between structural, propulsion, electronic, and control systems.


  Each testing cycle contributes towards improving the understanding, functionality, and reliability of the aircraft.


  ## 08. Technical Competitions & Engineering Challenges


  Aerial Martial actively engages with competitive engineering platforms that challenge students to apply their knowledge under defined technical requirements.


  Participation in competitions such as SAEISS Design, Development and Competition (DDC) provides exposure to aircraft design challenges, engineering decision-making, practical development, and performance evaluation within a competitive environment.


  These experiences encourage technical discipline, teamwork, resource management, and the ability to deliver engineering solutions within established constraints.


  ## 09. Knowledge Development & Team Collaboration


  The club also functions as a practical learning environment where members develop technical understanding through collaborative projects and shared engineering experience.


  Students gain exposure to different aspects of aircraft development, learn from experienced members, and contribute to the collective progress of ongoing projects. Knowledge transfer and mentorship help maintain continuity across successive batches while encouraging members to explore new areas of aerospace engineering.


  **An Integrated Engineering Approach**


  The strength of Aerial Martial lies in the relationship between these different areas of work. Aircraft design, structural development, propulsion, electronics, and flight evaluation cannot be treated as isolated activities. Each discipline contributes to the performance and functionality of the complete system.


  By bringing these areas together through practical projects and competitive engineering challenges, Aerial Martial aims to provide students with an environment where engineering concepts are developed, implemented, evaluated, and continuously improved.


  From the first design consideration to the final stages of aircraft testing, the focus remains on learning through engineering and turning ideas into machines capable of flight.
