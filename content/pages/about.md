  body: >-
    Aerial Martial is the Aeromodelling Club of PES's Modern College of
    Engineering, Pune, established in 2016–17. We are a student-led engineering
    team dedicated to the design, development, and fabrication of unmanned
    aerial vehicles, bridging the gap between theoretical aerodynamics and
    practical aircraft engineering.


    Driven by technical curiosity and competitive ambition, we continually challenge ourselves through national-level engineering competitions, including SAEISS Design, Development and Competition (DDC). We strive to build more than aircraft — we build engineering capabilities, practical expertise, and a culture of innovation.
philosophy:
  - title: Design with Intent
    body: Every aircraft begins with a purpose. We approach design through
      engineering principles, informed decisions, and clearly defined
      performance objectives. Every configuration, component, and design choice
      serves a functional goal.
  - title: Build to Understand
    body: We believe engineering is best learned through application. From
      fabrication to system integration, hands-on development transforms
      theoretical knowledge into practical expertise and real-world
      problem-solving skills.
  - title: Test, Evaluate, Improve
    body: Every test is an opportunity to learn. We evaluate performance, identify
      limitations, and refine our designs through experimentation and iteration,
      treating challenges as essential steps towards better engineering.
scope: >-
  Our operations span the key disciplines of unmanned aircraft development,
  combining theoretical engineering with hands-on implementation.


  **01. Aircraft Design & Aerodynamics**


  Aircraft configuration, airfoil selection, aerodynamic analysis, and performance-oriented design.


  **02. Structural Engineering & Fabrication**


  Airframe development, material selection, structural design, manufacturing, and assembly.


  **03. Propulsion & Power Systems**


  Propulsion selection, motor and powertrain integration, and aircraft power management.
