The Aero India Student Expo represents the premier showcase for university-level aerospace engineering on the subcontinent. The AeroClub division has been officially selected to present our latest advancements in autonomous flight control systems alongside leading defense contractors and international aerospace firms.

### Exhibition Objectives

Our primary objective for this deployment is twofold: to demonstrate the structural viability of our in-house manufactured carbon composite chassis, and to showcase the real-time processing capabilities of our custom LiDAR-assisted avionics stack.

![ia](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581092160562-40aa08e78837?auto=format&fit=crop&q=80&w=800 "de")

### Personnel & Logistics

A specialized detachment of seven engineers, led by our Division Captain and Chief of Avionics, will be managing the exhibition booth. The team will be conducting live telemetry simulations and structural teardowns of the Valkyrie prototype directly on the floor.

Industry professionals, potential sponsors, and media personnel are encouraged to schedule a technical walkthrough with our team prior to the expo dates.
